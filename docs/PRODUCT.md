# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Volunteers (peronda) and community organizers (ketua_peronda, ketua_krt). Volunteers may be of varying ages (including older community members), needing high legibility, clear controls, and ease of use in dark or low-light situations (e.g., night patrols).

## Product Purpose
Modernize Voluntary Patrol Scheme (SRS) management by digitizing attendance, incident logging, shift scheduling, and emergency alerts, replacing physical, paper-based books.

## Brand Personality
Secure, official, and highly accessible. Focuses on clear typography, robust contrast, and intuitive, uncluttered layout to ensure trust and ease of use.

## Anti-references
- Nested card patterns that increase visual noise.
- Low-contrast or small text that compromises legibility.
- Cluttered, over-engineered dashboards with excessive stats that distract from primary tasks.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity First**: The user's current status and primary task must always be obvious.
- **Extreme Legibility**: Strong visual contrast and readable font sizes to accommodate volunteers of all ages under low-light conditions.
- **Intentional Structure**: Clean card structures and flat, semantic layouts rather than nested containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Strict adherence to WCAG contrast guidelines (>= 4.5:1 for body copy). Clear tap targets (>= 48x48px) and support for larger default system text sizes.
